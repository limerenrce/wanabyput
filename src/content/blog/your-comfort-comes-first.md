A spa experience should leave room for a conversation. You do not need to know the right words or arrive with an understanding of every treatment name. A simple question is a good place to begin.

## Before an appointment

When a spa is taking reservations, ask about the experience before choosing it. Find out what it involves, which products are used, and what you will be asked to wear. Share preferences or concerns that are relevant to your comfort.

If you have questions about whether a treatment is appropriate for a health condition, seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on a service description.

## Boundaries are always welcome

You can ask for more coverage, a different pressure, a quieter room, or a pause. You can also say that a particular area should not be touched. A preference does not need a long explanation.

Clear communication is part of the experience. You are not interrupting it by asking for an adjustment.

## Arriving in your own way

Choose clothing that feels comfortable. Leave jewellery or valuables somewhere secure when practical. Check the spa's own arrival guidance rather than assuming that every place follows the same routine.

There is no need to turn the visit into a performance of relaxation. It is fine to ask questions, take a little time, or decide that something is not for you.

## Afterwards

Give yourself a moment before moving into the next part of your day. If you enjoyed an aspect of the experience, or something could have felt more comfortable, sharing it can be useful.
