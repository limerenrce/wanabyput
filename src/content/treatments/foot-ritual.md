## The luxury of doing less

The foot ritual is a concept for a gentle, uncomplicated pause. A comfortable seat, carefully folded linen, and a moment without an itinerary set the scene.

Rather than making the day busier, the intention is to create a little room in it. Final service details and any products used will be introduced once the collection is confirmed.
